# Dungeon Journal Enhanced

**Dungeon Journal Enhanced** adds quality-of-life options to [DungeonJournal](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/dungeonjournal) by Exehn, the boss, loot and quest journal for WoW Forever.

## __Features__

🟢 **__List View__**: Switch the dungeon list between the usual cards and a compact list of short bars, each with the dungeon's level range in front of its name. Many more dungeons fit on screen at once.

🟢 **__Sort by Level or Name__**: Sort the dungeons by level (the default) or alphabetically. The < > arrows beside the Dungeons button step through the dungeons in the same order.

🟢 **__Right-click to Hide__**: You can right-click a dungeon on the home page to hide a dungeon faster.

![image](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1821689/description_7bea2a08-e326-4ec7-93e8-59512429f289.png)

_Both buttons sit at the right end of the journal's home page header, next to Hide Dungeons. Your choices are saved for all your characters._

## __Requirements__

[DungeonJournal](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/dungeonjournal) by Exehn. Dungeon Journal Enhanced is a separate addon that plugs into it. It isn't made by or affiliated with DungeonJournal's author.

## __Languages__

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese, following the language chosen in DungeonJournal.

## __If DungeonJournal updates__

DungeonJournal updates often. If an update changes something this addon relies on, the journal keeps its normal layout and a one-line message in the chat says the dungeon list couldn't be arranged.
